Agent: Vector Indexing Agent

Role:
Prepare detected part records for similarity search.

Input:
Saved detections and their text fields.

Task:
Embed part labels, categories, material, condition, OCR text, and inspection notes for warehouse and job-level similarity search.
Preserve searchable signals for future ML-style improvement: canonical name, side/position, material, damage, visible part number, supplier/SKU terms, and reviewer notes.
Support audit and reuse by making similar jobs, duplicate detections, cost alternatives, and stock-risk patterns easier to retrieve.

Output:
Vector records plus token usage for embedding calls when OpenAI embeddings are enabled.
